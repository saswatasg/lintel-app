Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: The Lintel app does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What it hears, reads and shows is processed on your Mac, including speech recognition and AI summaries.

Data that stays on your Mac

  • Clipboard history, notes, teleprompter scripts, shelf items and focus sessions are stored only in Lintel's folder on your Mac. You can pause or clear clipboard history at any time; copies marked as passwords by password managers are never recorded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you dictate with voice typing, while a meeting transcript you started is running, or when the teleprompter follows your voice. Speech is turned into text on your Mac, by Apple's speech models and by the language models Lintel downloads. Audio is never saved or sent. During a meeting transcript, the last few minutes of audio are kept in memory to improve the text, then discarded.
  • Audio from other apps: Lintel reads app audio to set each app's volume and, when you transcribe a meeting, to hear the other side of the call. Nothing is recorded; a meeting transcript keeps only the text.
  • Meeting transcripts and summaries are saved as text in Lintel's folder, or in a folder you choose. If you choose a folder that a service like iCloud Drive syncs, that service syncs it, not Lintel.
  • Apple Intelligence: meeting summaries and dictation clean-up use Apple's on-device model.
  • Calendar and Reminders: with your permission, Lintel reads upcoming events to show your next meeting, and keeps your todos in the Reminders app (which syncs them through your Apple Account if you use iCloud). Lintel doesn't store or send your events.
  • Keyboard and Accessibility: with your permission, Lintel watches only for its own shortcuts (like the key you hold to dictate) and the volume and brightness keys, and uses Accessibility to paste your dictated text where you're typing. It doesn't record what you type.
  • Browser tabs: if you turn it on, Lintel checks which website is open in your browser, to notice distracting sites during a focus session and to spot calls like Google Meet. Addresses are never stored or sent.
  • AI tool usage: Lintel reads token counts from Claude Code and Codex logs on your Mac to show your usage. Their content isn't stored or sent.
  • Camera: used only while the Mirror tab is open, to show your own preview. Video is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Music and Spotify: with your permission, Lintel reads what's playing and sends play, pause and skip commands.

Network connections

Lintel connects to the internet only for the things below. Each connection goes straight from your Mac to the service named, never to the developer:

  • Speech and language models: Apple's speech models download from Apple. The optional Hindi, Bengali and English language models download from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you choose to add them.
  • Website icons for sites in your Launcher and on your focus block list, fetched from those websites. Meldstead, the developer's own web app, is in the Launcher by default (you can remove it), so its icon is fetched from meldstead.com in the same way. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Album artwork for the song playing in Spotify.
  • If you choose to connect them: your own AI tool plan usage from the provider you signed in to, and your Google Calendar events if you sign in to Google. Sign-in tokens are kept only on your Mac and are used only to show you that information.
  • Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Lintel only learns which plan is active; Apple's privacy policy covers payment.

As with any download, these services see your Mac's IP address and what was requested, under their own privacy policies. Lintel sends no information about you or your use of the app to the developer or to any third party: no analytics, no crash reports and no advertising.

Google Calendar

If you sign in to Google in Lintel, it asks for read-only access to your calendars and for your email address. Lintel uses them only to show your upcoming events in the notch and which account is connected. Events go straight from Google to your Mac and are not stored, shared or used for anything else. The sign-in token is kept only on your Mac, and signing out deletes it. Lintel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website and launch emails

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. If you choose to enter your email address to hear when Lintel is available, it is sent to Kit (kit.com), the email service we use, and stored there. It is used only to send you emails about Lintel: Kit first sends a message asking you to confirm, and every email has a link to unsubscribe. We never sell or share your address. To have it deleted, unsubscribe from any email, or reply to one and ask. Kit's handling of your address is covered by Kit's privacy policy.

Children

Lintel does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
