Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: Lintel does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What it shows is processed on your Mac.

Data that stays on your Mac

  • Clipboard history, notes, teleprompter scripts and shelf items are stored only in Lintel's folder on your Mac. You can pause or clear clipboard history at any time; copies marked as passwords by password managers are never recorded.
  • Calendar: with your permission, Lintel reads upcoming events from the Calendar app to show your next meeting. Events are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Camera: used only while the Mirror tab is open, to show your own preview. Video is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you turn on the teleprompter's “Follow voice” mode. Speech is recognised on your Mac; audio is never stored or sent.
  • Music and Spotify: with your permission, Lintel reads what's playing and sends play, pause and skip commands.

Network connections

Lintel connects to the internet only to fetch things you asked to see:

  • album artwork for the song playing in Spotify;
  • icons for websites you add to the Launcher;
  • if you choose to connect them, your own AI-tool plan usage from the provider you signed in to, and your Google Calendar events if you sign in to Google directly. Sign-in tokens are kept only on your Mac and are used only to show you that information.

Lintel sends no information about you or your use of the app to the developer or to any third party.

Children

Lintel does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
