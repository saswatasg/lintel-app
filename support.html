Support

Get in touch. Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/saswatasg/lintel-app/issues. We usually reply within two working days.

Getting started

After installing, Lintel lives in the notch at the top of your screen and in the menu bar. Rest the pointer on the notch, or press ⌃⌥Space, to open it. On a Mac without a notch it appears as a small pill in the same place.

Common questions

The teleprompter doesn't follow my voice. Turn on “Follow voice” in the Prompter tab and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition when macOS asks. Speech is recognised on your Mac.

Music controls don't work. Allow Lintel to control Music or Spotify when macOS asks, or in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

No meetings show in Next Up. Allow calendar access when asked. Calendars added to the Calendar app, including Google accounts in System Settings → Internet Accounts, appear automatically.

Keyboard shortcuts. ⌃⌥Space opens the island, ⌃⌥V the clipboard, ⌃⌥P the teleprompter and ⌃⌥N notes. You can change them in Settings.
